# Whether a row's log cannot say which account is the channel

Only without the configured account, where the channel is guessed from
who wrote its fields: two accounts that did leave the log unable to say
which writes are the round's, and every caller holds the row. With the
account configured there is nothing to guess,
[`log_channel_accounts()`](https://ubesp-dctv.github.io/ubep.azure/reference/log_channel_accounts.md).

## Usage

``` r
log_channel_unknown(channel, channel_account)
```

## Arguments

- channel:

  What
  [`log_channel_accounts()`](https://ubesp-dctv.github.io/ubep.azure/reference/log_channel_accounts.md)
  answered.

- channel_account:

  What it was asked with.

## Value

`TRUE` when the channel is not known.
